## Decisions

- **The wade.** The painting shows no way at all from the camp to the city: open water with the broken pier's last posts
  lies between them. The pier's line is traced as shallows: 'w' at x 13-17, y 4, a reed tile at (18,4), and 'w' at
  x 11-14, y 5 around Corvus. That is 10 walkable tiles over open water. The map would not work without them. It is the only
  crossing the painting does not show. The header says so, and so does "Needs from the lead".
- **The chain.** GLOOM_PATH has `bell-hearth` before `mh-salvage`, and the Fire goes at the dry right-hand belltower.
  So the chain has to stand between the Fire's side of the city and both the Belfry stair and the south exit. The only
  place the painting allows is the main street, just below the avenue. This keeps `test/maps.test.mjs`'s "the stair
  down to the Drowned Belfry lies past the salvage camp's chain, in the old city".
- **The guards' facing.** Each guard stands at its gate's end, in line with it, as before. Each faces the way the party
  comes. That was west for the old east-west street, and it is north now, so `mh-salvage`'s `face` changed from w to n.
  The ringers already faced n.
- **The break** (26,17): the one place where the flooded streets meet the main street. In the painting it is a narrow
  gap of reeds and water between the street wall's pillar and the arch ruin, one tile wide.
- **The camp's deck.** The painted upper deck straddles rows 4 and 5, with its planks at about y 4.0-5.3. I traced row 4
  as the deck and row 5 as its rails and posts, with the two stairs (x 5 and 9) and the deck's west end walkable. So
  the plank walk is one tile wide, as painted. The weak link is x 9-10 on row 4, under the crane's net, where the deck
  narrows to meet the pier head. It is walkable because the painted deck runs through there.
- **Props.**
  - The crane is in the painting (on the camp's deck), so `mh-crane` is a painted sign with the text: "The Tallymen's
    crane, lashed to the deck with tarred rope. A net hangs from its jib, heavy with what the water gave up: roof-lead,
    green bronze, the clapper of a bell as long as your arm."
  - The painting shows no diving bell (the crane holds a cargo net), so `mh-diving-bell` is removed.
  - The sealed chest keeps its sprite, on the jetty.
- **Edges closed.**
  - The lane's north end has a ruined wall at the top edge.
  - The drowned street's run off the bottom edge (x 15-16) is deep water.
  - Everything else on an edge is water or wall.
  - Only the three exits are open.
- **Shut-in ground traced solid.** The chapel's roofless floor in the south-east is walled in on every side. The flooded
  court behind the terrace stays walkable, because the terrace stair reaches it.
- **Tiles:** ':' old paving (the stairs too), ',' weeds and planted ground, 'w' flooded streets and the wade, '~' deep
  water, '"' reeds, '_' the camp's decks, 'b' the pier, the jetty and the bridges, '|' the crane, rails and posts,
  't' crates, 'T' trees and bushes, '#' ruined masonry, 'H' tower tops and tents, 'Y' columns and pillars, 'o' fallen
  masonry and the fountain, 's' the belltower's stair and door.
- **map-draft.** `--reach=all` finds no walkable tile out of reach. The lint's only note is 43 one-wide tiles: the
  camp's plank walk, the wade, the lane's stub, the stairs and the break. None of them is in a roam rect.
