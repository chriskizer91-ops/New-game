## Bogmire (42x28, was 34x28; 36.6 painting px a tile)

What the painting shows: a stilt town over black water with lily pads and dead trees. There are plank streets on
piles, timber houses under thatch and patched shingles, rope rails, and lanterns on poles. The big moot-hall (top
left) has a porch and a stair down to the west street. The market square in the middle has four striped stalls
round a plain centre. A boardwalk climbs north-east past a small watch-hut to the east edge. The north road runs up
to the top edge. The south-east road passes a house on stilts to a landing at the east edge. A south street runs
from the square, past a long house and a net shed, down to the bottom edge. The east quarter is rotten: broken planks
and bare stilts, with a rope across the end of the net-mender's porch.

## The Long Boardwalk (74x20, was 56x18; 51.2 painting px a tile, three panels)

What the paintings show: one long walkway of weathered planks on stilts over open black water. Rope rails run along
both sides and lamp-posts along the north rail. Reed islets stand out in the water, some with a twisted tree or
rocks. Panel a has a side jetty south to a fishers' platform and an open thatched shelter, with a boat moored beside
it. Panel b has a short jetty north to a landing with a moored skiff; where it meets the boardwalk, the planks are
patched with mismatched barge-planks. In panel c, the boardwalk runs east into the mist.

Where everything is now:
- The deck: rows 6-7 from x 0 to 26 (the painting's planks run higher in the west third), rows 7-8 from x 27 to 73.
  It is two tiles wide all along.
- exit `lb-w` [0,6,0,7] (to Bogmire, `from-boardwalk`); anchor `from-bogmire` (1,7,'e').
- exit `lb-e` [73,7,73,8] (to Misthollow, `from-boardwalk`), with `gate`, `sealed` text and hint unchanged; anchor
  `from-misthollow` (72,8,'w').
- `lb-lights` pack (17,13), face 'n': on the fishers' platform.
- `lb-shelter-chalk` sign (23,14), look 'plaque', text unchanged: at the shelter's open front, east end.
- `lb-broken-middle` gate [39,7,39,8], look 'barge-planks': across the deck at the patched barge-planks (37-40, 7-8),
  where the north jetty meets the boardwalk.
- `lb-drowned` block (38,6), face 's': on the foot of the north jetty. As before, it stands one tile west and one
  north of the gate's top tile.
- `lb-skiff-dock` lock (40,4), `blackwater`: the black water at the skiff's stern, off the jetty's east side.
- `lb-skiff` chest (40,3): aboard the skiff, beyond the dock. The dock is the only way to it.
- `lb-lights-1` (67,7), `lb-lights-2` (69,8), `lb-lights-3` (71,7) (props, `if` unchanged): over the deck near the
  east end.
- `roads` unchanged: `from-bogmire` -> `lb-e` through `lb-broken-middle`.
- `roam`: max 3 (kept), rects [2,6,25,14] (the west deck, the side jetty and the platform; it holds the pack's home),
  [27,7,38,8] (the deck from the turn to the patch), [40,7,72,8] (the deck past the gate, to the east end).
- `lore`: [[292,540,1,9],[332,668,71,9]] (scaled from (1,8) and (54,8)).

Decisions:

  beside it, off the road. While the gate is shut, the north jetty and the skiff are theirs, as the pier was before.

- Every reed islet is solid (`o` rocks and mud, `T` trees, and `~` for the reeds out in the water). None holds

- The lamp-posts are `*` on the rail line (rows 5-6). The shelter's back wall is `#` (it is hung with nets) and its
  roof `H`. The shelter has no door tile: its front is open.
- No prop without an `if`, so nothing is made `painted` or removed. The marsh-lights, the gate, the lock, the chest,
  the sign and the fights keep their sprites.
