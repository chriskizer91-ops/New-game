## The Lanternfen (45x30, was 40x32; 34.1 painting px a tile)

patches, and lanterns hung on posts. A road of trodden earth comes in at the south-west corner and winds north-east
to the top edge; a sunken hut with a deck, a jetty and steps stands in the reeds at the west; an old plank bridge
carries the road over the pools; a great dead tree stands on an island in the middle; a lamp-post and another great
dead tree pinch the road just below the north-east exit.
