a side path runs south outside the stones into the willows, to a small clearing by the water.

Decisions:
- The seven always-lit ward-stones stand on stones the painting shows, so they became painted signs (no sprite).
  Their name is "The ward-stone" (the A button reads "A · The ward-stone", like "A · The carved stone").
- `wm-stone-sw` is load-bearing: it stands in the only gap between the inside of the ring (the south-west hut's west
  garden) and the side path. Both twins are signs, so one of them always closes it. Were it made walkable, the side
  path would join the village: the ward-gate would no longer hold the road and the witch-ward would no longer guard
  the clearing (`road.test`, `maps.test` would fail).

- Faces kept as they were (only at/area changed). Sedge's `face: 's'` looks over the water from the deck's end; she is
  talked to from the deck (west).
- One-wide corridors (lint note, 35 tiles): the plank walks and the garden strips between the huts. Willowmurk has no
  roamers (`roam: null`).
