## The Murkway: 45x36 (was 44x40)

The two panels, `map-murkway-a.png` at x 0-23 and `-b.png` at x 21-44, are blended over x 21-24. One tile is 42.7 of the
painting's px. `overTiles: false`. Painting: 1440x1152, 791.2 KB of WebP (0.49 bytes a px; the test allows 0.5).

What the painting shows: a grey cliff along the top with two stone stairs. Below the western stair (the fen stair) a path
of trodden earth runs south. It crosses three plank bridges to a stepping-stone ford, then a fourth bridge and a long
boardwalk. It climbs through the reeds where the halves meet and crosses a bridge to a fork, where the eastern stair's
path comes down. A last bridge leads past a hummock with two standing stones, and the path runs to the south-east corner.
There is bare peat along the way and a big peat south of the boardwalk. Everything else is black water, reed-beds, dead
trees, scrub and stones.

Tiles: `=` the path, `b` the bridges and the boardwalk, `w` the ford's stepping-stones, `s` the stairs, `.` tussock, `,`
marsh flowers, `"` reeds, `m` bare peat, `~` black water, `T` willows and dead trees, `t` bushes, scrub and reed-beds
standing in water, `o` stones, stumps and logs, `^` the cliff.

Decisions:
- **The second, older stair** (30-31, 0-3): its top two rows are cliff (`^`), so it cannot be climbed. Its two lower steps
  and the path below stay walkable. `mk-old-stair` is a new id that the lead asked for. It is a painted sign on its highest
  step (30,2), with the text: "An older stair than the fen stair climbs this cliff, but its top fell with the rock long
  ago, and the steps now stop halfway up."
- **The bog locks lie on painted bare peat.** West of the path the painting shows no bare peat until below the second
  bridge. So `mk-bog-west` lies at (9-10, 11-12) and `mk-bog-east` at (13-15, 6-7). Both are on the first stretch below
  the stair, one on each side of the path. E2E 29 steps into `mk-bog-west` at (9,11) from (9,10).
- **`mk-bog-end`** used to be near the road's end. The painting has no bare peat near the south-east exit, so it lies on
  the peat strip beside the eastern stair's path. `mk-bog-shrine` and `mk-bog-camp` lie on the two peat patches of the
  road's last stretch.
- **Small peat patches with no lock** are plain `m` tiles: (1,9-10), (16-17,6), (20,18), (7-9,21), (12,23), (10-11,26),
  and row 34 around the chest. Each lock is one rectangle of walkable tiles, and the spec fixes the lock ids, so I added
  no lock.
- **Sprites kept:** the painting shows no boat, signpost, tally-post, boundary stone, ward-stone or marsh-lights. So
  `mk-wreck`, `mk-sign`, `mk-boundary`, `mk-tally-post`, `mk-ward-stone` and both `mk-lights-*` keep their sprites. The
  shrine keeps its `reed-shrine` hearth look, because the painter left the hummock plain for it. No prop was made
  painted or removed.
- **The road is 2 tiles wide or more everywhere:** the stair, every bridge, the boardwalk, the ford (3 lanes) and the
  cut. The map-draft lint lists seven 1-wide tiles, all on reed edges or the west bank, none on the road: (4,7), (34,7),
  (1,11), (41,14), (32,15), (16,19), (20,25). No walkable tile is unreachable (`--reach=all`). Every edge is closed
  except the two exits.
