# 06 — Assets on hand, and the build in milestones

## Paintings already made and traced (the old game, branch `claude/cool-ptolemy-uc93gg`)

The old game holds **56 painted walkable maps** (27.6 MB as stored there), each already traced into a walkable grid,
plus two Hearth cut-scenes. Most are canon places, so they can be walked in Thareia; their old-game content (who
stands where, what the fights are) is replaced, and each grid is re-checked against its painting.

| Region | Old paintings that fit Thareia |
|---|---|
| Gloomfen | Bogmire, the Long Boardwalk (3 panels), Rotbridge, the Blackwater Causeway, Willowmurk, the Murkway (2), Misthollow, the Drowned Belfry, the Lanternfen, the Mother's Hollow, the Blackwater Reach, the Tidal Flats |
| Verdant Wilds | Thornhollow, the Thornway, Eldergrove, the Heartroot (2), Mosswatch (2), Mossfall, the Hindwood, Fawnrest, Briarmaw's Den, and the six painted road maps (the Old Bridge, Plankford, Drystone Lea, Shrinewood, Silverfall, the Last Camp) |
| Mirrordeep and the Keep | the Keep, the Keep hall, the Keep galleries (3), the Hearth Road |
| Sunscorch | Sandspire, the Sun Road, the Dust Trail, the Glass Flats, Dusthaven, the Deep Shaft (2), Miragewell, Scorchgate, the Scorchgate Vaults |
| Ironspire | Ironhold, Ironhold Deeps, the Iron Stair, Harrow's Forge, Peak's Veil, Stormwatch, the Frost Road, Frostmere, Beneath Frostmere, the Rockslide Pass, Highfold |

New paintings for Thareia so far: the town square, Bogmire's walking view (`../art-in/scenes/walk-bogmire.webp`), the
stream ruins, the temple courtyard, the night graveyard, four battle backdrops and one cut-scene.

**Size:** everything above will not fit in one 30 MB file at full quality alongside the new art. Each chapter takes
the maps it needs, re-stored at a lighter quality where the difference does not show (the demo's battle backdrop
proved small images look right once the game pixelates them).

## The build, in milestones

Like the old game, the build goes chapter by chapter; each milestone is a playable file for the phone.

| Milestone | Content |
|---|---|
| **T1** | The engine moved over: the travel layers (world map, region flight, walking maps), the ship, the new sounds and music, the Thareia party, save slot. The Prologue in Bogmire and the first flight. |
| **T2** | Chapter 1, the Verdant Wilds (levels 1–10), the rented skiff |
| **T3** | Chapter 2, the Keep (10–18), your own skiff |
| **T4** | Chapter 3, the Sunscorch (18–28), the refitted skiff |
| **T5** | Chapter 4, the Ironspire (28–36), the cruiser |
| **T6** | Chapter 5, the Gloomfen's depths and the Southern Lowlands (36–42), the Warming, the Aethership |
| **T7** | Chapters 6 and 7: the Crossing and Auros (42–50), the ending |
