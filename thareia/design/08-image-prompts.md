# Image prompts: T1 finish and T2 (Chapter 1, the Verdant Wilds)

Paste each prompt as it is. Save each picture as the file name given, and send them in any order. **Must** means the
game is waiting on it. **Nice** means it improves things, but the game works without it.

**The style line** (already in every prompt): *painted fantasy JRPG art in the style of Final Fantasy IX, rich warm
painterly detail, soft light, no text, no UI, no people unless the prompt asks for them.*

**The sizes**
- **Walking maps** are seen from high up at a slight angle (the same view as your Bogmire docks picture), at 1536 × 1024.
  Paths, decks and floors must be clearly visible and unbroken, because I trace them tile by tile.
- **Battle backgrounds** are side-on at eye level, 1448 × 1086, with the bottom third open and flat for the fighters to
  stand on.
- **Cut-scenes** are painted moments with no party members in them, 1448 × 1086.
