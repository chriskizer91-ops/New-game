# Batch 3: the Gloomfen Marsh

16 paintings: every map of the Gloomfen, and the Gloomfen Gallery. The loop and the naming are as in `README.md`. For each one: attach its
reference from `batch-3/refs/` as the first picture and your painting `art-in/pilot/map-thornhollow.png` as the second (the style your
whole-map paintings matched), paste its prompt, generate, and keep the best try under the file name given. A map too
big for one picture comes as panels (`-a`, `-b`, ...) that overlap; paint them the same way and the game joins them.
Upload the finished pictures (or one zip) to `art-in/batch-3/`, or send them in the chat.
