style and the naming are as in `README.md`. For each one: attach its reference from `batch-2/refs/`, paste
its prompt, generate, and keep the best try under the file name given. A long road comes as two panels
(`-a` and `-b`) that overlap by a few rows; paint them the same way and the game joins them.
Send the finished pictures back in the chat (say which is which), or upload them to `art-in/batch-2/`.

**Your own look is welcome.** You told me you edited the pilot prompts to make them look better, and it
showed. Change the style line to the look you want, as long as you keep two things: the first paragraph
(keep the layout exactly), and the "Do not include" list. The game now shows your paintings in full
detail, so detail is worth having: ask for at least the size each prompt names, bigger if you can.
