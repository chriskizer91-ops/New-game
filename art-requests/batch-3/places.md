# The Gloomfen's places (batch 3), for tools/paint-prompts.mjs

One section per painting, in the order the page lists them. The game draws people, foes, chests, gates, locks,
fires, signs and props on top of the painting, so a place where one stands is painted as the ground under it.

## map-murkway-a.png
Map: murkway · The Murkway · fen · the western half
The place: The Murkway, Willowmurk's safe paths through the bog, western half. At the top edge a grey cliff with stone steps coming down it (the fen stair from Mossfall). Below it the bog: black pools with ripples, reed-beds, tussock grass, clumps of bog-willow and alder, and patches of wet black-brown peat (keep every patch of bare mud where it is: it is sucking bog). A winding path of trodden earth and old planks runs south through it, with a shallow ford; keep it open. Low mist over the water, cold and still.

## map-murkway-b.png
Map: murkway · The Murkway · fen · the eastern half
The place: The Murkway, eastern half, painted exactly like the western half it overlaps: the same black pools, reed-beds, tussocks and bog-willows, the same peat patches. The safe path of trodden earth and old planks winds from the cliff stair at the top down to the south-east corner, where it leaves for Willowmurk; a small reed shrine stands on a hummock beside it (leave the hummock's top plain: the shrine's lamp goes there). Keep the path open all the way.

## map-willowmurk.png
Map: willowmurk · Willowmurk · willow-village · the hidden village
The place: Willowmurk, a hidden village of reed-thatched huts under great weeping willows, on islets of dark earth joined by plank walks over black water. A broad road of packed earth crosses the village from the east edge to the west edge, over plank bridges. In the middle, a moot-circle of old flagstones (leave its centre plain: the village's hearthfire goes there). A ring of standing ward-stones around the village, and at the south-west a side path out past the stones into the willows. Soft green-gold light through the leaves, fireflies, peace with an edge of unease.

## map-rotbridge.png
Map: rotbridge · Rotbridge · channel · the one crossing of the Blackwater
The place: Rotbridge: the Blackwater Channel, wide, slow and black, runs north to south through the middle, crossed east to west by one ancient timber bridge on stone piers, its old carvings weathered. On the east bank a toll-house of dark timber with a slate roof, and the road running up to the bridge (leave the road at the bridge's east end plain: Hodge's striped toll-bar goes there). On the west bank the road on to Bogmire, a small jetty, and a reed islet out in the water. Grassy banks with scattered trees; mud flats and reeds in the shallows below the bridge. Grey, damp, a little menacing.

## map-bogmire.png
Map: bogmire · Bogmire · stilt-town · the stilt town
The place: Bogmire, a ramshackle town on stilts over black water: plank streets on piles, weathered timber houses with patched roofs, a big moot-hall, rope rails, lanterns on poles. A market square of planks in the middle (leave its centre plain: the town's hearthfire goes there). Plank roads lead out north, north-east, east and south-east. In the east quarter the stilts are rotting and a street is closed off. Warm lantern light against cold water, busy and poor.

## map-lanternfen.png
Map: lanternfen · The Lanternfen · bog · the eastern bogs
The place: The Lanternfen, Bogmire's eastern bogs: open black water and wet ground, dead trees standing grey and bare, reed tussocks, a road of trodden earth winding from the south-west corner up to the north-east. At the west, a sunken old hut in the reeds. Small patches of peat. Paint it clear and readable (the game lays its own fog over it); a few faint lights hang over the water. Eerie, silent, sad.

## map-mothers-hollow.png
Map: mothers-hollow · The Mother's Hollow · drowned-grove · a drowned grove
The place: The Mother's Hollow: a drowned grove of black willows in a ring around a long, sunken house of dark timber whose windows glow warm (every lamp in it lit). Dark wet ground, roots, moss, a little black water between the trees, a path coming up from the south edge to the house's door. Leave the ground in front of the house plain: the game draws the Lantern Mother there. Paint it evenly lit so that every root and board reads clearly (the game lays its own darkness over this place).

## map-long-boardwalk-a.png
Map: long-boardwalk · The Long Boardwalk · boardwalk · the west third
The place: The Long Boardwalk, west third: a long walkway of weathered planks on stilts, running east to west over open black water, with lamp-posts along it. A side jetty runs south to a fishers' shelter on stilts. Small reed islets with a lone twisted tree stand out in the water. Grey sky light on dark water.

## map-long-boardwalk-b.png
Map: long-boardwalk · The Long Boardwalk · boardwalk · the middle third
The place: The Long Boardwalk, middle third, painted exactly like the other two panels: the same planks on stilts over black water, the same lamp-posts and reed islets. In the middle the boardwalk is broken and patched with mismatched barge-planks (keep it walkable). A short jetty runs north to a moored skiff's landing stage.

## map-long-boardwalk-c.png
Map: long-boardwalk · The Long Boardwalk · boardwalk · the east third
The place: The Long Boardwalk, east third, painted exactly like the other two panels: the same planks on stilts, lamp-posts and reed islets, and at the east edge the boardwalk runs on into the mist toward the drowned city.

## map-misthollow.png
Map: misthollow · The Misthollow Ruins · sunken-city · the sunken city
The place: The Misthollow Ruins, a city the marsh swallowed: grey stone towers leaning out of the water, arches, half-drowned streets of cobbles, small squares with arcades, a canal with a little stone bridge, and two belltowers, one with a stair going down. On the west, a broken pier over the water and a Tallyman salvage camp of timber decking and a crane. Paint it clear and readable (the game lays its own fog over it). Cold blue-grey stone, green water-stains, drowned grandeur.

## map-drowned-belfry.png
Map: drowned-belfry · The Drowned Belfry · belfry · beneath Misthollow
The place: The Drowned Belfry, a bell-hall under the city: a nave of big old flagstones between rows of pillars, green water pooling at the sides, a carved wooden choir-screen across the middle, and an apse at the north end where the great bells hang (leave the apse floor plain). A stair comes in at the south edge. Green water-light on wet stone. Paint it evenly lit so that every flagstone reads clearly (the game lays its own darkness over this place).

## map-blackwater-reach.png
Map: blackwater-reach · The Blackwater Reach · channel · the lower channel
The place: The Blackwater Reach, the lower channel between reed-banks: a broad road of trodden earth along the north bank (the towpath) from the east edge to the west edge, a beached boat hull on the bank, a drowned mill, a small dark pool in the reeds, and the black channel filling the middle with sunk boats in it. Reed-beds and willow scrub along the south bank. Grey light, heavy and quiet.

## map-tidal-flats.png
Map: tidal-flats · The Tidal Flats · mudflat · the channel mouth
The place: The Tidal Flats, where the channel meets the sea: wide grey-brown mudflats with tide-pools, old wrecks half sunk in the mud, and the shallow grey-green sea all round. In the middle the Tallymen's barge-camp: timber decking, tarred sheds, a beacon, and a great iron chain running west out into deep water to a post at its end. A road of planks and packed mud comes in from the east edge. Wind, gulls' country, salt and rust.

## map-causeway.png
Map: causeway · The Blackwater Causeway · causeway · the way home
The place: The Blackwater Causeway: a raised road of old stone setts with long kerb stones, crossing the shallows of Mirrordeep from the south-west corner to the north-east corner, the water just fallen from it (dark water-marks on the stones). Reeds and a few small islets of grass along it; open shallow water everywhere else. Clear morning light after rain.

## map-keep-gallery-3.png
Map: keep-gallery-3 · The Gloomfen Gallery · keep · the reliquary's fourth room
The place: The Gloomfen Gallery, the fourth room of the Keep's reliquary, painted like the Ironspire Gallery next door: a torch-lit gallery with walls of weathered grey granite, torches on the north and south walls, a door in the middle of the west wall, a floor of worn flagstones and a long runner of honey-brown planks down the middle (keep the floor bare: the game stands its relic pedestals there). Moss-green cloth hangings instead of the other rooms' blue.
