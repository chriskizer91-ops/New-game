# Act III's places (batch 4)

Milestone 7's four maps lie under Hearthstone Keep, in a new region, the Hearth Below. Each is painted from the words
below, with no layout reference, and then traced (`game/docs/M7-SPEC.md` A10, §6.3): the map takes the painting's
shape and puts its road, doors and places where the painting has them. So the words say where things go, but the
painter decides how they look. The game draws people, foes, chests, gates, fires and signs on top, so wherever one
stands, the painting shows plain ground.

The grid in "Scale" is only for proportion. It is never drawn, and the traced map may use a different one.

## map-hollow-hall.png
Map: hollow-hall · The Hollow Hall · council · the Council's First-Age chamber, under the Keep's vault
Shape: landscape 3:2 · Scale: 36 squares across, 24 down
The place: The Hollow Hall, the Council's First-Age chamber deep under Hearthstone Keep, older than the Keep above it: a long, solemn hall of dark grey granite with a violet cast, its walls carved with worn hearth-flames and the marks of the four regions. Braziers burn low along the walls. Dust and cold ash lie on everything. Four great stone chairs stand empty, each glowing faintly violet-black, as if something dark sat in it a moment ago. Ancient, solemn and wrong.
Layout:
- The way in: at the bottom edge, near the left end, a stair of worn steps (3 squares wide) comes down into the hall from the vault above.
- The nave: from the foot of that stair, a nave of big old flagstones, 5 squares wide, runs the whole length of the hall to the right end. It runs between two rows of massive pillars, one row along the top side and one along the bottom.
- The four chairs: behind the pillars, in four bays spaced evenly along the nave, four great stone chairs stand on low daises facing the nave. From left to right:
  - the first, on the top side, carved with a great tree;
  - the second, on the bottom side, carved with a sun;
  - the third, on the top side, carved with an anvil under a mountain;
  - the fourth, on the bottom side, carved with a lantern among reeds.
- The far end: at the right end, a great round stone table carved with a hearth-flame, with the nave passing round it. Beyond it, by the right wall, a broad stair (3 squares wide) goes down through the floor into a red glow.
- An alcove: a small alcove in the top wall past the fourth chair.
Leave plain: the nave's floor in front of each chair (the game draws a line of soot there, with the chair's owner beside it); the alcove's floor (a chest); the floor at the foot of the stair in.

## map-ash-stair.png
Map: ash-stair · The Ash Stair · ash · the hearth's roots, going down
Shape: portrait 2:3 · Scale: 24 squares across, 36 down
The place: The Ash Stair, where the Eternal Hearth's roots go down through the world. It is a deep shaft of grey rock, its walls wrapped in roots of black iron as thick as towers, twisting down like a great tree's roots. Seams of glowing ember run through the rock like veins, and grey ash lies drifted on every step and ledge like snow. Hot, hushed, going down.
Layout:
- The way in: at the top edge, left of centre, a stair comes down from the hall above.
- The stair: a broad stair of rough-cut steps (4 squares wide), hewn along the shaft's walls. It winds down in long switchbacks from the top edge to the bottom edge and never breaks. It goes round an open drop in the middle that glows red from far below.
- The first landing: in the top third, on the right, a wide landing with a deep round niche in the rock wall behind it, as big as a cart.
- The narrows: just below the first landing, the stair squeezes to 3 squares between two great iron roots.
- Two wide landings, where the stair turns further down, each about 10 squares across: one on the left in the middle of the picture, and one on the right in the lower third.
- A side ledge: off the middle landing, a narrow ledge runs along the wall to a small flat spot.
- The way on: at the bottom edge, the stair runs on down out of the picture into a red glow.
- Everything that is not stair, landing or ledge is solid: rock, iron roots, or the drop.
Leave plain: the niche's floor (the game draws a great cold coal there, the Under-Coal); the narrows (the game draws the ash-men who hold them); the side ledge's flat spot (a chest); both wide landings (foes walk there).

## map-chained-deep.png
Map: chained-deep · The Chained Deep · chains · the cavern of the First Sleeper
Shape: landscape 3:2 · Scale: 42 squares across, 28 down
The place: The Chained Deep, a vast cavern at the roots of the world, floored with fused black slag cracked with faint red. In the middle lies the First Sleeper, the oldest thing in Aethermoor. It is curled asleep in a great hollow in the floor, like a low hill of dark stone and old ember, one enormous eye shut, its heart glowing slow and red through its hide. A web of great iron chains, each link the size of a cart, pins it down, staked into the rock all round the hollow. From the cavern roof, the Eternal Hearth's black iron roots come down and sink into its back, like a tap in a tree. Three of the great chains run from the web across the cavern floor into tunnels in the walls: one to the lower-left corner, one to the middle of the bottom edge, one to the lower-right corner. Vast, still and ominous, with a heartbeat you can feel.
Layout:
- The way in: at the top-left corner, a stair comes down from the Ash Stair above.
- The road: from the foot of that stair, a walkway of old iron plates riveted into the slag (4 squares wide), with a low chain rail on its lower side. It runs right along the top part of the cavern, above the Sleeper's hollow, then bends down to the forge door on the right edge. Keep it open and continuous, with nothing across it.
- The narrows: about halfway along, the walkway squeezes to 3 squares between a spur of rock and the hollow's rim.
- The Chain Fire: past the narrows, a small round platform beside the walkway, under a broken chain that hangs from the roof.
- The forge door: in the middle of the right edge, a great iron door in the rock stands open, red-gold light spilling out, with the walkway running through it.
- The Sleeper, its hollow (about 16 by 10 squares), the chains and their stakes are solid: nobody walks there.
Leave plain: the narrows (the game draws the foes who hold them); the platform's centre (the game draws a fire in an iron brazier); the paving in front of the forge door (someone waits there).

## map-worldforge.png
Map: worldforge · The Worldforge · forge · the Unsmith's forge at the bottom of the world
Shape: landscape 3:2 · Scale: 36 squares across, 24 down
The place: The Worldforge, the Unsmith's forge at the bottom of the world: a colossal forge-hall hewn into the rock and plated with black iron. Against the right wall stands the Worldforge itself: a furnace as big as a house, shaped like a great heart of black iron and firebrick, its open mouth blazing white-gold, with channels of molten metal running from it across the floor. Around it:
- a great anvil the size of a cart;
- racks of giant tongs and hammers;
- chains and hooks hung with broken relics waiting for the fire;
- heaps of slag, and sparks drifting.
Heat haze makes the air shake. Terrible and magnificent: the work of a master smith who has gone wrong.
Layout:
- The way in: in the middle of the left edge, a walkway comes in through an iron door from the Chained Deep.
- The moat: a river of molten metal in a stone channel, 3 squares wide, runs from the top edge to the bottom edge about a third of the way in from the left, cutting the hall in two.
- The bridge: one iron bridge, 3 squares wide, crosses the moat in line with the walkway.
- The forge floor: beyond the bridge, a wide floor of iron plates and hammered stone, open for walking. Smaller molten channels run along its edges from the furnace to the moat.
- The smith's place: in the middle of the forge floor, before the furnace, an open space about 5 squares across, with the great anvil to one side of it.
- The heart: at the foot of the furnace, a raised step before its blazing mouth.
Leave plain: the bridge's near end (the game draws the forge's guardian there); the smith's place (the game draws the Unsmith there); the step before the furnace's mouth (the game marks the forge's heart there).

## cut-ending-rekindle.png (optional)
Still: the Rekindle ending · Shape: landscape 3:2
The place: the Great Hall of Hearthstone Keep. The Eternal Hearth burns gold and steady again in its great carved fireplace, as it has for nine hundred years. The four council chairs are filled again, and the townsfolk have gathered, warm and relieved. In the foreground, small and seen from behind, stand the four heroes:
- an armoured young warrior with a sword;
- a small halfling scout in a hood;
- a lanky scholar in bark-brown robes with a ringed staff;
- a priestess in white robes with a blue sash and a shepherd's crook.
But in the firelight, faint and huge on the walls, lie the shadows of great chains. Warm, safe and bittersweet: the world as it was, and what it costs.

## cut-ending-release.png (optional)
Still: the Release ending · Shape: landscape 3:2
The place: the Great Hall of Hearthstone Keep at dawn. The Eternal Hearth is dark and cold for the first time in nine hundred years, with only ash in its fireplace. The great doors stand open. Through them, far off across the hills, four colossal shapes of stone and ember walk away into the sunrise, free, trailing broken chains. The townsfolk each hold a small candle or lantern of their own. The four heroes stand in the doorway, seen from behind, watching them go. Cold grey-gold light; sombre and hopeful.

## cut-ending-anew.png (optional)
Still: the Kindle Anew ending, the true ending · Shape: landscape 3:2
The place: the Great Hall of Hearthstone Keep. The Eternal Hearth blazes with a new fire, gold at its heart and edged with every colour: rose, green, sea-blue and white. In the heart of the fire a single weapon glows like the sun: the Warden's own masterpiece. An old iron poker leans against the hearth beside it. Broken chains lie on the floor. Through the high windows, four great shapes of light rise from the earth into a dawn sky, free. The four heroes stand before the fire, seen from behind, and the whole hall is lit gold. Triumphant, warm and new.
