# Art pilot: four images

Four images to find out, before we commit to dozens, how the paintings look in the game: two maps
(the first place you see and the first town), one cut scene and one portrait. Upload them to
`art-in/pilot/` as `README.md` explains.

| File | What | Shape | Attach |
|---|---|---|---|
| `map-keep.png` | Hearthstone Keep, the first map | landscape 3:2, at least 1536 × 1024 | `refs/ref-keep.png` |
| `map-thornhollow.png` | Thornhollow, the first town | square, at least 1024 × 1024 | `refs/ref-thornhollow.png` |
| `cut-blue-hearth.png` | The prologue: the Eternal Hearth turns blue | landscape 3:2, at least 1536 × 1024 | nothing |
| `portrait-hilda.png` | Hilda the smith, for her dialogue | square, at least 1024 × 1024 | nothing |

## 3. `cut-blue-hearth.png`: the prologue

Nothing to attach. Paste:

```
A cut-scene illustration for a 16-bit JRPG, in detailed pixel art.

The moment: the Council hall of Hearthstone Keep. A vast, old stone hall with tall pillars, faded banners showing a flame over a castle, and a long oak council table strewn with papers and grain ledgers. At the far wall stands the Eternal Hearth, a great carved stone fireplace whose fire has burned for nine hundred years, and right now, mid-sentence, it has turned an eerie, cold blue. Every torch along the walls has gone blue too. Around the table sit five elderly Elders in heavy robes, frozen mid-argument, their faces lit blue. One man is already on his feet: Fenwick the hearthkeeper, an old man with cropped silver hair and a short silver beard, a plain wool tunic under a worn leather tabard, a ring of iron keys at his belt. He does not look surprised. He looks caught.

Composition: seen from the far end of the table, with the blue hearth as the bright focal point in the centre, Fenwick standing in the middle ground and the Elders around the table. Keep the hearth and Fenwick in the middle band of the picture, because wide screens crop the top and bottom.

Style: 16-bit SNES-era JRPG cut-scene pixel art, crisp pixels, dramatic lighting: cold blue firelight against warm old stone and wood, deep shadows. A warm epic, with an edge of dread.

Do not include: text, captions, speech bubbles, UI, borders, frames, watermarks.

Landscape 3:2, at least 1536 x 1024 pixels.
```

**Optional, if your generator can edit a picture:** attach your favourite `cut-blue-hearth.png` and
ask: *"The same picture a moment earlier: every flame a normal warm gold, the Elders arguing, Fenwick
still seated. Change nothing else."* Save it as `cut-gold-hearth.png`. The prologue then starts gold
and turns blue.
