# Batch 4: the Hearth Below (Act III)

Four maps are needed for Milestone 7: the places under Hearthstone Keep where Act III is fought. There are also four
optional stills. The loop and the file names work as in `README.md`, with one difference: **there is no layout
reference.** You paint each place from its description, the way your batch-3 paintings were made. The game then traces
its map from your picture (`game/docs/M7-SPEC.md` A10). The descriptions say where the way in, the road and the way on
go, because the game walks on the picture, and what to leave as plain floor. Everything else about how it looks is
yours.

For each one:
1. Attach the style picture it names. They are your own paintings, so the new places match the old ones.
2. Paste the prompt and generate.
3. Keep the best try under the file name given.

Upload the pictures (or one zip) to `art-in/batch-4/`, or send them in the chat. Until they come, the game draws these
maps from its own tiles, so paint whenever you like.

**What matters most in a map:**
- The way through stays open, from the way in to the way on.
- The doors and stairs sit at the edges the description names.
- The spots it asks to leave plain are bare floor.

A try that walls off the road, or puts a door somewhere else, is the one to throw away. Small differences are fine:
the map is fitted to your picture, not the other way round. The words each place is painted from are also in
`batch-4/places.md`.
