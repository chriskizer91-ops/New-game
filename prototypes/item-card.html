    Bram Holloway

    Human Hearthguard · Level 8

    Prototype for the Aethermoor JRPG. Six foes, each stronger than the last, each guarding one item from Common to Mythic. Every image is drawn by code when the page loads: an item is a short list of shapes and materials, and a small renderer turns it into a shaded, outlined 64×64 portrait, a 16×16 bag icon, and the smaller copy your hero holds. After the Drowned Warden, “Keep fighting” rolls new foes and new weapons from the same recipes with random shapes, metals, gems and affixes.
